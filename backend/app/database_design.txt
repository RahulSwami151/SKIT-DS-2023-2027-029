## Final schema summary
- `users` — Student accounts.
- `tasks` — Student tasks containing title, deadline, subject, task_type, priority, importance, mood, status, and sor_score.
- `tasks.student_id` → `users.id`.
- `task_dependencies` — Self-referencing dependency edges between tasks.
- `task_dependencies.task_id` → `tasks.id`.
- `task_dependencies.prerequisite_task_id` → `tasks.id`.
- `journal_entries` — Mood, stress, and energy check-ins.
- `journal_entries.user_id` → `users.id`.
